# LedgerLens — Design References & Taste Filters

> **IMPORTANT**: Use these references as **taste inspiration only**, not as direct templates or visual clones. LedgerLens must remain domain-specific as a financial reconciliation control room.

---

## 1. High-Density Operational Terminals (Calibrate Density & Confidence)
- **Bloomberg Professional Terminal / Palantir Foundry**: High information density, monospace precision, multi-source evidence layouts, calm dark backdrops.
- **Linear.app**: Restrained typography, keyboard-first navigation, subtle state indicators, zero visual noise.

---

## 2. Design System & Spec References
- **Ballance Design System Spec**: Token-driven, 7-state interaction models, explicit responsive rules, testable WCAG 2.2 AA acceptance criteria.
- **design.md Specifications**:
  - `https://github.com/voltagent/awesome-design-md`
  - `designmd.co`
  - `UXPlanet / DevCommunity`

---

## 3. Motion & Interaction Principles
- **Emil Kowalski Motion Principles**: `https://emilkowalski.ski/skill` & `https://github.com/delphi-ai/animate-skill`
  - Motion must be state-change driven (150ms–220ms, ease-out).
  - No perpetual ambient animations, no glowing gradient loops, no floating cards.
- **Design Motion Principles**: `https://github.com/kylezantos/design-motion-principles`
